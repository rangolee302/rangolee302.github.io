Terms

The writeups describe work that already exists. A store link means that listing was public on the project page. A prototype label means the build is not a finished product.

Sending an email, or opening the mail draft from the form, does not create a contract, a booking, or an obligation to reply by a set time.

Game names, store names, and engine names belong to their owners. They are used here to identify the work.
